# DataRepresentationCalculator

Data Representation in Computer Systems Convertor Twos,Ones Complement +Excess-M This Data Representation Calculator aka Binary Data Convertor is allowing to convert different computer data representations. It capable to convert Decimal, Binary, Hexadecimal, and Octal data into: Binary, Signed Magnitude, One's Complement, Two's Complement, and Excess-M Computer format binary strings of user's chosen length. This app can be helpful to students at their computer science classes and specially for those who at computer architecture and organization classes, or for anyone who is curious to see how computers are processing/handling numbers.
